## One table for everything

Pick a game, set the table rules, and play — against the computer instantly, or with friends in a **private room** that opens with a shareable code.

| Game | Players | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speed | 2 | Real time, no turns — first to empty their hand and call “Speed” wins |
| War | 2–4 | Highest card takes the pile; a tie starts a war |
| Yaniv | 2–6 | Discard sets and runs; call Yaniv at 7 points or less |
| Durak | 2–6 | Attack and defend — the last one holding cards is the Durak |
| Solitaire | 1 | Klondike, draw one or three, standard or Vegas scoring |
| President | 3–7 | First out is President; the last one pays tax next round |
| Shithead | 2–5 | Hand, face-up and blind layers — don't be the last one |

## Under the hood

- **Seven rule engines** with bots whose difficulty changes both their pace and the quality of their play.
- **Realtime multiplayer** with a host-starts flow and live turns between devices.
- **Expo + React Native** — the same TypeScript project runs as a website and as native apps.
- **Hebrew-first** with full RTL, and English available from settings.

## Hand-written logic, AI-accelerated

I designed the architecture and the game logic myself, and used AI coding agents to move faster — with type-checking, linting and tests as guardrails. The result: a product of this scope — seven rule engines, bots, realtime rooms and stats — built and launched by one developer.
