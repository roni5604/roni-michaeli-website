## Brief

A coach who teaches martial arts, ninja training, classes and workshops needed one place to send people — something that feels as energetic as his training and works perfectly on a phone.

## What I delivered

- A **mobile-first** React site with strong imagery and clear calls to action
- The **weekly schedule** and workshop offers front and centre
- Direct contact in one tap
- Hosting on GitHub Pages with his **own domain**
