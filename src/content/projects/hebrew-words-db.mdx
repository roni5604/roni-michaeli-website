## The gap

Every comprehensive Hebrew word list was either **AGPL** — which “infects” any software that links it — or **non-commercial only**. That makes them unusable for most real products, open-source or commercial. Hebrew NLP deserved better.

## What I built

A large, growing, plain-text list of Hebrew words: nouns in singular **and** plural, verbs conjugated across binyanim, tenses and persons, adjectives with gender and number agreement, adverbs, function words and current Israeli slang.

No definitions, no translations, no niqqud — **just the words**, because that's exactly what was missing.

## Why it matters for AI

- **Tokenizer and vocabulary work** for Hebrew AI language models
- **Training and evaluation data** for spelling, morphology and NLP tasks
- **Word games and autocomplete** — it powers the dictionary behind [Find the Word](/projects/wordbox)
