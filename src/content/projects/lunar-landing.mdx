## The challenge

Israel's Beresheet lander crashed on the Moon in 2019. This simulation asks: **what control logic would have landed it?**

## Approach

- A **PID controller** holds vertical speed to a target that shrinks with altitude.
- The spacecraft's **angle** is steered from 60° toward 0° as it closes in on the target.
- Extra **horizontal braking** kicks in below 3,000 m.
- Every step is logged — time, altitude, speeds, angle, fuel and throttle — and exported for analysis.
