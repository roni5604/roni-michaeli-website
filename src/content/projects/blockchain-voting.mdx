## Why a blockchain

Votes inside a company only matter if everyone trusts the count. Putting them **on-chain** makes every vote transparent and immutable.

## What the contract does

- **Roles** — managers create and close votes; customers participate
- **Votes** — created, joined and closed entirely on-chain
- **Treasury** — company funds and withdrawal requests, with events such as `UserAdded`, `VoteCreated` and `FundsAdded`

The React front end talks to the contract through **MetaMask**, and the whole system runs locally on Ganache for testing.
