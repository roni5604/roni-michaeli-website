## What it does

For every frame of a video, the detector finds ArUco markers and annotates each with its **ID**, **distance from the camera** and **yaw angle**, outlining it in green.

It also writes a CSV with the marker's 2D corners and 3D pose — **distance, yaw, pitch and roll** — so the results can drive a navigation algorithm, not just a demo.
