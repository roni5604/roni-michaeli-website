## From 2D to 3D

The first version moved a drone on a plane with simple sensor models and platform search. Going 3D meant rethinking almost everything:

- **Movement** in a full volume, not a plane
- **Sensors** that see above and below, not just around
- **Autonomy** that weighs vertical risk as well as horizontal
- **Visualisation** that makes the drone's view understandable

## How it decides

The drone casts rays to simulate its sensors, scores the **risk** of each direction — including up and down — and chooses a safe heading. It explores autonomously and **returns home** to recharge.
