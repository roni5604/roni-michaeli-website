## What it does

- **Tracks** the ISS in real time using Skyfield and live TLE data.
- **Shows** what its camera would see, by streaming KML into Google Earth every few seconds.
- **Two modes** — a static north-down view, and dynamic targeting where the station locks onto ground targets and adjusts heading and tilt.
- **Dashboards** — a Tkinter simulation GUI for rates and energy use, and a PySide6 3D viewer for attitude.
