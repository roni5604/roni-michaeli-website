## Two sides of one app

**Students** see upcoming classes, register in a tap, manage their profile and rate the studio.

**Managers** create classes, see who registered, upload or remove gallery photos and grant manager access to other accounts by email.

Built as a team of three — Roni Michaeli, Roy Wasker and Elor Israeli.
